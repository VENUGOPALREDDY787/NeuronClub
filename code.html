🔐 Decode the Mystery

The result is written before the tale is told,
A single letter, not words, when the winners hold.

What you cannot see, you still must hear,
The year of dominance will become clear.

Fix that year in the heart of your key,
Neither first nor last is where it should be.

To close the code, don’t search too far,
Count eleven ahead from where you are.
